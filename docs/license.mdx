# License

Hangar is free on any Mac used by one person, under the [Hangar Small Business License](../LICENSE.md).

A company can also run it on shared machines and build servers with up to **100 CPU cores in total**. More than that
needs a separate license from Baseguard.

| You are… | |
|---|---|
| One person on your own Mac | Free |
| A company, shared machines and build servers up to 100 CPU cores in total | Free |
| A company above 100 CPU cores | Needs a separate license from Baseguard |

The [license text](../LICENSE.md) is the one that counts; this page is a summary. Third-party software in Hangar is
listed in [THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md](../THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md).
